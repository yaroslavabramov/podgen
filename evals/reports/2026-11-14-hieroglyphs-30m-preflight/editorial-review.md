  and subsequent plan must receive the new duration consistently; the accepted

  Ask research to develop documented hypotheses, tests and corrections, especially

  verdict. Alternatively, the planner can select other supported material.

  or placeholders despite `available`. S9 (Yale) supplies a concrete translation
  and later correction; S47 supplies the large primary-text OCR of *Précis*;
  claims selected from it need an edition/page locator and readable evidence.
- Read the new plan together with its separate review. Assess whether its
  substantive developments can sustain the duration; do not prescribe acts or
  dialogue in advance.

  budgets before generation. Saved draft input reached 43,168 tokens and first

  rewriting are the principal capacity uncertainties. These measurements do not

Neither a word target nor a plan's time allocation proves final audio duration.

## Only the complete result can establish

- **Middle and development:** the reasoning continues to change after the name
  comparison, with substantive evidence rather than length supplied by digression.
- **Transitions:** the listener understands why the next document or test is
  needed and follows time changes without reconstructing the chronology.
- **Clarity and repetition:** sign comparisons work without pictures; repeated
  explanations and caveats earn their space. The accepted short script's reversed
  “молоко” analogy and repeated Hartleben attribution deserve attention.
- **Ending:** the main question resolves without a succession of extra endings;
  the distinction between a working method and complete translation remains clear.
- **Whole-episode coherence:** read the entire final script, not just changed
  passages or the reviewers' acceptance decisions; then listen to the complete
  audio, including pacing, pronunciations and joins.
- **Actual duration/cost:** measure the assembled WAV and elapsed stages; account
  for generation, counting, cached input, retries and reused paid work separately.
  Retain unknown research cost as unknown. Do not extrapolate a total from the
  shorter run or use offline replay time as generation time.

These are review questions, not new model-facing prohibitions or acceptance gates.
The evidence does not yet justify choosing 60/90 minutes or rewriting the workflow
to produce the script in parts; that decision follows the full 30-minute result.
