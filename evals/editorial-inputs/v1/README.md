# First editorial input format

[GPS](gps-clock/) and [Snow](snow-pump/) each have a `shared.json` assignment and dossier plus a `fixed-plan.json` control. The controls record plans prepared for development on November 5. For a fresh Snow run, use the [current own-plan case](../current/snow-pump/README.md). The historical Snow assignment lost the brief's detective genre and its fixed plan opens with the pump handle and the earlier fall in deaths.

The shared inputs carry the question, audience, format, host roles, facts, mechanisms and sources. The factual content of former discussion threads lives on the cards. The own-plan route reads only the shared material. The fixed route also verifies the shared digest and the original plan. Selected excerpts keep their original Unicode character offsets and content hashes. [Shared schema](../../../backend/schemas/editorial-input.v1.schema.json) · [Fixed-plan schema](../../../backend/schemas/fixed-editorial-input.v1.schema.json) · [Frozen originals](../../cases/development/README.md)

**GPS.** Eight cards cover the signal, pseudorange, common clock, joint solution, geometry, a microsecond of error, other errors and known altitude. The clock-bias sign, independent observations, measurement epoch and distinction between range and position error matter to the explanation. The roughly 300 m figure follows from the speed of light. The formula on NASA slide 38 appears in the slide image. The lighthouse example in the fixed plan is a constructed illustration of the measurement model.

**Snow.** Sixteen cards distinguish observations and interviews in 1854, registration and death dates, fatal attacks, later investigations, the physical inspection and the disputed diagnosis. Their chronology establishes when events happened. The planner chooses when the audience learns about them.
