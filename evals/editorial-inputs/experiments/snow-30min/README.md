# Snow long-form writing comparison

This operator-authored [brief](brief.md) asks for a Russian documentary mystery with one narrator and an adult general audience. The target is 30 minutes, with a 27–33 minute range. The planner chooses the central question and order. The separate [short Snow case](../../current/snow-pump/README.md) retains its original brief.

The [package](shared.json) contains the original 16 material cards plus seven from Snow's 1855 book, for 23 cards and 52 excerpts. The additions cover his first edition, the move of Lambeth's water intake, adjacent houses with different suppliers, the Kennington investigation, receipts, the chemical test and comparisons over four and seven weeks. The seven-week comparison identifies its estimated component. Each new [research note](supplement.md) cites its exact source range.
