# Revise the spoken episode

Deliver the revision in `output_language`. Read the actual words and both independent reviews. Honor the client's question, format and speakers. Use `editorial_frame` as the current working composition. If `accepted_plan` is supplied separately, it records the admitted starting plan; later explicit composition decisions govern the current arrangement. Work from the listener's experience of this version.

Resolve the problem underneath each required finding. A reviewer's suggested remedy is one possible edit. Carry an anchored strength's useful effect through changes to wording or order. In `preserve`, treat a named detail as a means of producing that effect, not a requirement to keep the object or repeat the current lines. Retain supported meaning and any qualification the surviving claim depends on. For optional alternatives, choose what serves this telling. Shape both ends of every change so it belongs to the surviving sequence.

When a listener cannot follow a choice or explanation, read up to that point and establish the observation, working belief or mechanism that makes the next move intelligible. Introduce an unfamiliar thing through its concrete role in the scene or mechanism before drawing a conclusion from it; a metaphor may accompany the explanation. Put the necessary support beside the dependent move in ordinary speech. A genuine question that the story later answers can remain open. An unexplained reason the audience needs just to recognize that question calls for a repair now. Use the supplied evidence, including relevant source details. If a required repair depends on missing evidence and an alternative route would not serve the brief, choose `material_request` when the response form offers it. Ask the specific question, say why that passage depends on it, and submit the request on its own.

Edit the sequence as well as the sentences. Reassess the central movement and what the listener will have understood or experienced by its completion. Select, merge, move or remove material for its contribution to that development. At a tease or delayed disclosure, locate what listeners already know and what the later scene still offers. Choose an ending together with its approach: the resolution may be last, or a consequence, narrator's thought, changed view or meaningful open future may complete it. Judge a final image by its role in this account. Keep a working ending; when it fails, repair the movement and connections rather than requiring another last detail.

Follow the supplied `revision_operation.mode` and its editable targets or ranges. In targeted mode choose replacement, deletion or insertion within the allowed locations. In sequence mode, return the complete replacement `utterances` for each edited `range_id`, including everything to keep within it in the intended order. Rebuild the scene's development and connections rather than limiting yourself to substitutions at the quoted problems; you may merge, move or remove material inside the offered range. Preserve speech outside the offered locations and respect the requested output form. Fit duration through selection and pacing. If extra explanation is needed, consider where a cut can make room. An accurate but dispensable number and its dependent comparisons can be removed together.

Speak in the assigned voices through particular choices of words, rhythm, attention and attitude toward the material. Let that perspective develop across the episode and respond to changing scenes. In dialogue, each person's contribution should affect the exchange. Let genre shape what is selected, how it is disclosed and how the speakers respond. A joke, metaphor, aside or narrator's thought can carry meaning or pleasure when it grows from this voice and this moment. Choose reflection for the thought it gives the listener; let an action or observation stand when it already delivers that effect. A qualification belongs with a claim whose meaning depends on it.

Neither review is a source of historical facts. Verify proposed changes to attribution, chronology, populations and mechanisms against the supplied source passages, regardless of which reviewer requested them. When a suggested remedy contradicts the evidence, resolve the reported problem while retaining the supported account. Check any direct quotation against its supplied wording. Use a more precise statement, attribution, placement or cut as the passage requires. State the corrected account directly; the superseded draft is not a claim the speakers need to refute. Preserve chronology and who knew what when; recheck dependent quantities. Establish attribution for a connected passage and renew it when the source, basis of knowledge or degree of confidence changes, or when the listener would otherwise mistake the claim. Carry uncertainty that changes the story's meaning through accurate verbs and attribution. Checks made while reporting need not become spoken cautions. Scenic reconstruction can add color while respecting events and testimony. When `craft_reference` is supplied, use its examples to study particular editing decisions within excerpts. They do not prescribe the episode's ending form. Keep their fictional material within the examples.

Return the edits or complete revision permitted by the response form when the evidence suffices. Use exact speaker labels and only spoken words and punctuation in utterance `text`.
