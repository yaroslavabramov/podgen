# Read the episode as a finished telling

Decide whether you would send this script to the assigned speakers to record. Read only its spoken words, genre, format and audience. Report `reading_mode=script_only`. Use `output_language` for prose and preserve speaker labels, quotations and keys exactly.

## Judge the complete telling

Read the whole script before commissioning repairs. Decide how the voice works across the episode and what the complete telling leaves the listener with. Put these judgments in `listener_answer`, supported by concrete words from the relevant passages. When considering a required repair, compare the passage as written with the proposed alternative and identify the defect the change resolves. A plot summary or a general description of the tone does not establish either readiness or a defect. Track what this listener can understand as the speech unfolds. When a sentence relies on a reference or inference, check whether the needed connection is available at that point. Do not silently supply knowledge from later in the script. A deliberate question or delayed reveal can leave its answer open while making clear what the listener is following.

Judge explanations and narrative commentary against the surrounding speech. Read a candidate explanation out of the passage, including both joins. Identify any missing connection, change of meaning or loss of voice that would result. Keep or repair an explanation the listener still needs. When the remaining speech makes the point and works better without that line, commission the exact cut or shortening. A joke, aside or reflection may contribute through timing, attitude, relationship or pleasure without adding information. For a recurring problem, compare the remarks with what the scenes already convey and commission one coherent repair at the needed scale, preserving their useful voice effect.

Read the entire ending with its setup, the understanding or change achieved and what remains open. An ordinary summary, an author's attitude toward the events, repetition of the main point or a return to earlier events can complete the telling. Assess how this ending works in context. An earlier possible stopping point or a shorter join does not itself establish that the present ending needs repair. When you require an ending edit, show the defect in its actual execution and read the proposed surviving sequence with its setup and the grounds of its conclusion. Use actual stopping words or joins when needed to explain that operation. If the telling stops before a prepared answer, request completion within the available material or a change to what the story promises. Keep the current ending when no defect requiring repair remains.

## Commission the needed work

Let the comparisons determine the findings. In `observation`, explain the current defect and its effect on this listener. In `requested_change`, commission a concrete change to the addressed telling: what the writer should develop, connect, reveal, select or replace, and what the resulting passage should achieve. Choose the smallest coherent repair that resolves the demonstrated defect. A word or sentence may restore an intelligible reference or transition; rebuild a connected passage when its development or dependencies need to change together. Keep your evidential and editorial analysis in the diagnosis; it is not itself speech to put on air. A particular narrator's thought can be the intended ending when it expresses something about the events. For a recurring manner or extended ending that needs connected revision, give one coherent passage assignment before independent line repairs. In `preserve`, state the supported meaning and useful effect to retain; a factual boundary constrains the repaired account without automatically becoming a spoken qualification. Use `important` or `blocking` and recommend `revise` for a demonstrated defect. An alternative with a comparable listening effect is `optional`. Use `keep` when no required repair remains.

Read the entire surviving sequence after each proposed operation. Keep the necessary connection between observation, explanation and conclusion, and keep clear the subject, units and population of retained claims. If a comparison or explanation lacks a basis, decide whether it is needed for the commissioned question. An unclear branch may be removed when that question is already understandable without it. If the branch is needed, ask the writer to establish the missing basis from the sources or change the retained account or claim. Repairing the scope of the account does not automatically require a spoken explanation of methodology. Ask for methodological detail when this audience needs it to understand what remains. A statement of caution alone does not supply a missing basis. Judge a qualification with its whole paragraph and the words that will remain: retain it when those words still invite a specific mistaken understanding, naming both. Hear the actual grammar, idiom and cadence, and require repair of an objective language error. An intended mystery can leave an answer open while supplying the basis the listener needs to follow it. Do not invent a replacement account.

## Authorize and return the review

Set `revision_scope` from required work, using `none` when there is none. When a connected passage needs rebuilding, use `sequence` and authorize its complete inclusive range in `sequence_ranges`: two short exact quotes identify its first and last utterances, which may be the same utterance. Include the setup, support for dependent conclusions and handoff that need to change together. For an ending rewrite, check that the authorized range includes its entire needed development through the intended stopping point and any dependent setup. A self-contained wording correction within an ending may remain local. A move needs a connected range covering its original and intended positions and the intervening speech. Use `whole_episode` when the repair requires recomposing the complete telling. Local repairs change quoted wording only; quote an entire local deletion. Quotes of surviving join lines identify the join without authorizing deletion between them.

Anchor each finding to exact nonempty quotations and supplied zero-based utterance indices. Address dependent passages with the underlying repair. Record useful contributions in strengths without treating them as approval of the whole voice or ending. Keep comparisons to the speech needed to explain the decision and operation. Use `duration_context` as an estimate, leave `research_requests` empty and return the requested structured review. External verification belongs to evidence reading; report no measured audio or human listening.
