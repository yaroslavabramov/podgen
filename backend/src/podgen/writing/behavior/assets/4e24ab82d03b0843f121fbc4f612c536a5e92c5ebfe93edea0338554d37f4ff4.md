# Revise the spoken episode

Deliver the revision in `output_language`. Read the actual words and both independent reviews. Honor the client's question, format and speakers. In `editorial_frame`, the current composition may differ from the earlier plan. Work from the listener's experience of this version.

Resolve the problem underneath each required finding. A reviewer's suggested remedy is one possible edit. An anchored strength tells you what contribution to carry forward while changing the words or order. For optional suggestions, decide what the particular passage gains. Shape both ends of each change so it belongs to the surviving text.

When a listener cannot follow a choice or an explanation, read up to that point. Find the observation, working belief or mechanism that makes the next move make sense. Put the needed support beside that move, in ordinary speech. A genuine question that the story later answers can remain open. An unexplained reason the audience needs just to recognize that question calls for a repair now. Use the supplied evidence, including relevant source details. If a required repair depends on missing evidence and an alternative route would not serve the brief, choose `material_request` when the response form offers it. Ask the specific question, say why that passage depends on it, and submit the request on its own.

Edit the sequence as well as the sentences. Check what each episode contributes after the preceding one. Merge, move or remove repeated work. At a tease or delayed disclosure, locate what the listener already knows and what the later scene actually delivers. Restore an early-spent discovery at the first words which disclose it. If the answer is already clear before the closing passage, choose a last action, image or thought that adds something; cut the extra wrap-up. Retain callbacks, humor, reflection and useful orientation where they earn their place.

Follow the supplied `revision_operation.mode` and its editable targets or ranges. In targeted mode choose replacement, deletion or insertion within the allowed locations. For a sequence repair, reshape its development and connections. Preserve untouched speech and respect the boundaries of the requested output. Fit duration through selection and pacing. If extra explanation is needed, consider where a cut can make room. An accurate but dispensable number and its dependent comparisons can be removed together.

Give each speaker an idiomatic, audible voice. Locate commentary on the telling itself: announcing a genre, certifying the narrator's care, rebutting an unmade claim, or telling listeners how impressed to be. Where it merely repeats the work of the scene, let a particular observation, action or line of thought carry that moment. A distinct voice, aside or joke can deserve to stay.

Verify factual repairs against the supplied evidence and any source wording. Use a more precise statement, attribution, placement or cut as the passage requires. Preserve chronology and who knew what when; recheck dependent quantities. Scenic reconstruction can add color while respecting events and testimony. When `craft_reference` is present, draw on its editorial choices, keeping its fictional material out of the episode.

Return the edits or complete revision permitted by the response form when the evidence suffices. Use exact speaker labels and only spoken words and punctuation in utterance `text`.
