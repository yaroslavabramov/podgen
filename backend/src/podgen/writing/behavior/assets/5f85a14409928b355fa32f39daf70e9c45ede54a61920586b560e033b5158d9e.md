# Revise the spoken episode

Deliver the revision in `output_language`. Read the current script and the compact editorial assignment. Honor the client's question, format and speakers. Use `editorial_frame` as the current working composition. If `accepted_plan` is supplied separately, it records the admitted starting plan; later explicit composition decisions govern the current arrangement. Preserve the current telling's intent while carrying out the assigned changes.

For each required instruction, locate the exact passage it addresses and establish what must change in the spoken result. Carry out that repair within the authorized locations, then read the resulting passage against the requested effect. Resolve the underlying problem rather than merely acknowledging it. Retain the stated supported meaning, necessary factual conditions and useful contribution; a named detail may change when its contribution survives. Shape both ends of the change so it belongs to the surviving sequence.

When a listener cannot follow a choice or explanation, read up to that point and establish the observation, working belief or mechanism that makes the next move intelligible. Introduce an unfamiliar thing through its concrete role in the scene or mechanism before drawing a conclusion from it; a metaphor may accompany the explanation. Put the necessary support beside the dependent move in ordinary speech. A genuine question that the story later answers can remain open. An unexplained reason the audience needs just to recognize that question calls for a repair now. Use the supplied evidence, including relevant source details. If a required repair depends on missing evidence and an alternative route would not serve the brief, choose `material_request` when the response form offers it. Ask the specific question, say why that passage depends on it, and submit the request on its own.

Keep the episode's central movement and current completion except where an assigned repair calls for changing them. For a structural task, select, merge, move or remove material for the requested result and read the altered development with its preceding and following speech. An ending repair may finish at the resolution or through a consequence, narrator's thought, changed view or meaningful open future. Choose the stopping point for what it completes in this telling.

Follow the supplied `revision_operation.mode` and its editable targets or ranges. In targeted mode choose replacement, deletion or insertion within the allowed locations. In sequence mode, return the complete replacement `utterances` for each edited `range_id`, including everything to keep within it in the intended order. Rebuild the scene's development and connections rather than limiting yourself to substitutions at the quoted problems; you may merge, move or remove material inside the offered range. Preserve speech outside the offered locations and respect the requested output form. Fit duration through selection and pacing. If extra explanation is needed, consider where a cut can make room. An accurate but dispensable number and its dependent comparisons can be removed together.

Speak in the assigned voices through particular choices of words, rhythm, attention and attitude toward the material. Let that perspective develop across the episode and respond to changing scenes. In dialogue, each person's contribution should affect the exchange. Let genre shape what is selected, how it is disclosed and how the speakers respond. A joke, metaphor, aside or narrator's thought can carry meaning or pleasure when it grows from this voice and this moment. Choose reflection for the thought it gives the listener; let an action or observation stand when it already delivers that effect. A qualification belongs with a claim whose meaning depends on it.

The editorial assignment is not a source of historical facts. Verify proposed changes to attribution, chronology, populations and mechanisms against the supplied source passages, regardless of their editorial origin. When a requested repair contradicts the evidence, resolve the reported problem while retaining the supported account. Check any direct quotation against its supplied wording. Use a more precise statement, attribution, placement or cut as the passage requires. State the corrected account directly; the superseded draft is not a claim the speakers need to refute. Preserve chronology and who knew what when; recheck dependent quantities. Establish attribution for a connected passage and renew it when the source, basis of knowledge or degree of confidence changes, or when the listener would otherwise mistake the claim. Carry uncertainty that changes the story's meaning through accurate verbs and attribution. Checks made while reporting need not become spoken cautions. Scenic reconstruction can add color while respecting events and testimony.

Return the edits or complete revision permitted by the response form when the evidence suffices. Use exact speaker labels and only spoken words and punctuation in utterance `text`.
