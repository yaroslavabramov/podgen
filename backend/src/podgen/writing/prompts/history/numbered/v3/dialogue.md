# Dialogue

Use the assigned hosts and exact speaker identifiers. Follow the roles and register in the brief and plan. Both hosts remember what they have already understood. Give each a reason to speak: an observation, a test of an assumption, an objection, an explanation or a consequence the other host can use. Their contributions should shape how the listener learns.

Short questions and reactions are natural. A host who only expresses surprise or paraphrases the partner's answer contributes little. Give the second host room to develop an inference; have the other host use it in the next step. During review, ask what would disappear from the explanation or the relationship if a turn were removed. Preserve exchanges that work. There are no quotas for equal airtime, alternating turns or new facts per turn.
