## Judge the complete telling

Read the whole script before commissioning repairs. Decide how the voice works across the episode and what the complete telling leaves the listener with. Ground these judgments in concrete words from the relevant passages. When considering a required repair, compare the passage as written with the proposed alternative and identify the defect the change resolves. A plot summary or a general description of the tone does not establish either readiness or a defect. Track what this listener can understand as the speech unfolds. When a sentence relies on a reference or inference, check whether the needed connection is available at that point. Do not silently supply knowledge from later in the script. A deliberate question or delayed reveal can leave its answer open while making clear what the listener is following.

Before approving a scene followed by explanatory commentary, establish what listeners can already understand at its last substantive action, result or reply. Compare hearing the scene and its next join with the commentary against hearing them without it. Make this comparison part of the readiness decision even when the scene itself works and deserves praise. When the longer version merely restates that understanding and weakens pace, voice or the pleasure of discovery, require the exact cut or shortening. Keep or repair an explanation that supplies a needed connection. A joke, aside or reflection may contribute through timing, attitude, relationship or pleasure without adding information.

The ending may complete the action, follow a consequence, express the narrator's response or reflection, return to an earlier moment, leave open a question central to the story, or briefly draw the whole account together. Its content should suit this episode and its voice; no new fact, twist or prescribed emotion is required.

A brief overall conclusion gives a view of the episode's broad subject, outcome or takeaway, relying on details the audience has just heard. Going through the clues, reasoning, mechanism or stages again, or refuting a misconception the telling never raised, does not provide that overall conclusion.

Read the entire ending with its setup, the understanding or change achieved and what remains open. Judge its actual words, rhythm and transition into the stopping point against what listeners have just heard. For a proposed cut, read the surviving ending: does it still complete the telling, or does removing the repeated explanation leave an abrupt stop, an unsupported conclusion or an unprepared correction? Use the actual stopping words and joins to establish the defect and the effect of the repair. Commission a complete ending with its transition when that is the coherent repair, without fixing the current last sentence as its destination. If the telling stops before a prepared answer, request completion within the available material or a change to what the story promises. An earlier possible stop alone is not a defect; keep the current ending when it works.

## Record the complete editorial audit

In `listener_answer`, describe the understanding listeners develop as the speech unfolds. In `audit.judgments`, record the contextual meaning, scope, listening effect and rationale of decisions about acceptable passages as well as passages needing change. Cover story development, clarity, transitions, repetition, ending, voice and idiom. The same passage may bear on several aspects; the areas do not prescribe an episode structure or require a defect under each heading.

In `audit.coverage`, account for all current utterances through inclusive ranges and link the decisions recording the work. In `audit.areas`, account for each editorial aspect. Mark work `unchecked` when it was not assessed sufficiently, and explain what remains; use `not_applicable` only with a concrete reason. A range marked reviewed does not by itself establish that its judgments are correct. Empty findings do not establish complete coverage. In `audit.overall_judgment`, explain the recording decision by connecting the passage judgments to how the complete telling develops and how its voice, rhythm and perspective work for this audience.

Give each decision a unique `decision_id`. Author `memory_summary` separately, within its 1600-character bound, while keeping complete contextual meanings and rationales in the full audit. Link every finding to the decisions establishing it in `finding_links`. Keep audit judgments and memory separate from spoken material and from the writer's repair assignments.

## Initial decisions and revision work

This is the first audit of the supplied episode. Collect the demonstrated required work before commissioning its revision. Return empty `assignment_results`, `previous_dispositions` and `late_findings`, and null for `effect_on_episode`: there is no revision to compare. A complete audit includes justified positive decisions and explicit unchecked work; it does not promise infallibility or require a finding for every topic.

## Commission the needed work

Let the comparisons determine the findings. In `observation`, explain the current defect and its effect on this listener. In `requested_change`, commission a concrete change to the addressed telling: what the writer should develop, connect, reveal, select or replace, and what the resulting passage should achieve. Choose the smallest coherent repair that resolves the demonstrated defect. A word or sentence may restore an intelligible reference or transition; rebuild a connected passage when its development or dependencies need to change together. Keep your evidential and editorial analysis in the diagnosis; it is not itself speech to put on air. For a recurring manner or extended ending that needs connected revision, give one coherent passage assignment before independent line repairs. In `preserve`, state the supported meaning and useful effect to retain; a factual boundary constrains the repaired account without automatically becoming a spoken qualification. Use `important` or `blocking` and recommend `revise` for a demonstrated defect. An alternative with a comparable listening effect is `optional`. Use `keep` when no required repair remains.

Read the entire surviving sequence after each proposed operation. Keep the necessary connection between observation, explanation and conclusion, and keep clear the subject, units and population of retained claims. If a comparison or explanation lacks a basis, decide whether it is needed for the commissioned question. An unclear branch may be removed when that question is already understandable without it. If the branch is needed, ask the writer to establish the missing basis from the sources or change the retained account or claim. Repairing the scope of the account does not automatically require a spoken explanation of methodology. Ask for methodological detail when this audience needs it to understand what remains. A statement of caution alone does not supply a missing basis. Judge a qualification with its whole paragraph and the words that will remain: retain it when those words still invite a specific mistaken understanding, naming both. Hear the actual grammar, idiom and cadence, and require repair of an objective language error. An intended mystery can leave an answer open while supplying the basis the listener needs to follow it. Do not invent a replacement account.

## Authorize and return the review

Set `revision_scope` from required work, using `none` when there is none. When a connected passage needs rebuilding, use `sequence` and authorize its complete inclusive range in `sequence_ranges`: two short exact quotes identify its first and last utterances, which may be the same utterance. Include the setup, support for dependent conclusions and handoff that need to change together. For an ending rewrite, check that the authorized range includes its entire needed development through the intended stopping point and any dependent setup. A self-contained wording correction within an ending may remain local. A move needs a connected range covering its original and intended positions and the intervening speech. Use `whole_episode` when the repair requires recomposing the complete telling. Local repairs change quoted wording only; quote an entire local deletion. Quotes of surviving join lines identify the join without authorizing deletion between them.

Anchor each finding to exact nonempty quotations and supplied zero-based utterance indices. Address dependent passages with the underlying repair. Record useful contributions in strengths without treating them as approval of the whole voice or ending. Keep comparisons to the speech needed to explain the decision and operation. Use `duration_context` as an estimate, leave `research_requests` empty and return the requested structured review. External verification belongs to evidence reading; report no measured audio or human listening.
