# Review the proposed story

Walk the entire `candidate_plan` in order from the audience's supplied starting knowledge. Reconstruct its promise and answer. For each material step, check what the preceding plan has actually established, why the next question arises and whether its reasoning can use the concepts it depends on. Listing a prerequisite or mentioning a term does not establish its meaning or the relationship the next step uses. The brief's question alone does not ground the listener's interest or understanding. Inspect the opening, all acts and transitions, ending, host functions and duration assumptions.

Judge the intended form and audience: expert knowledge, a scene or deliberate unanswered mystery can be valid without an everyday example. A mystery may withhold its answer while making the question intelligible.

Check whether the handoff describes intended understanding and useful host contributions or already dictates finished speech. In dialogue, inspect recurring-response instructions as well. Request planning intentions where either would constrain the writer. Preserve explicit wording requirements and supplied quotations. An ending should specify what closes the question; the writer needs room to make it sound natural.

Check claims against the offered passages, including contrary evidence and dependence between accounts. Distinguish an event's date from when an actor learned about it, and a plausible mechanism from a measured effect. Check counted populations, periods, units and operations. Identify the conditions and uncertainty that affect each conclusion.

For each finding give its location, importance, observable problem, specific change, useful material to preserve and relevant support labels. Identify strengths worth retaining. Copy targets from `allowed_review_targets`, which address the proposal as stated in `review_target_root`. Choose the smallest relevant existing field; locate a missing step at neighboring fields or the promise it affects.

Choose `accept` when the complete ready route grounds its central question and material steps in knowledge available at the point of use, needs no required repair and has no blocking research. If the route relies on an unestablished premise, use linked targets across the opening, acts or transitions to request a route-level `revise`, not a cosmetic change to the opening. Facts already available call for editorial repair; `needs_research` is for a blocking evidence gap. Optional improvements allow acceptance. Preserve unresolved blocking requests. A null proposal needs research. Return the decision, concrete reasons, reconstructed answer, limits, strengths, findings, research requests and duration note.
