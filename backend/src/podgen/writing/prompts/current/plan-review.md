# Review the proposed story

Assess the exact `candidate_plan` against the assignment, dossier and attributed evidence. Reconstruct its promise and answer. Could its connected route prepare this audience to understand that answer in one hearing? Inspect the opening, every act, prerequisites, transitions, ending, host functions and duration assumptions. Identify missing explanatory steps rather than trusting the future writer to discover them.

Check substantive claims against the offered passages and qualifications, including contrary evidence and dependence between accounts. Card labels establish selection, not factual proof. Distinguish an event's date from when an actor could know about it, and a plausible mechanism from an established effect. Keep uncertainty proportional to the evidence.

For each finding give its location, importance, observable problem, specific change, useful material to preserve and relevant support labels. Identify strengths worth retaining. Copy targets from `allowed_review_targets`: these address the proposal itself, as stated in `review_target_root`. Use the smallest relevant existing field, including real neighboring fields for a missing step. Do not write a replacement plan.

Choose `accept` only when this complete, ready version needs no required changes and has no unresolved blocking research. Optional improvements do not require revision. Choose `revise` for repairs possible with supplied material, or `needs_research` for a blocking evidence gap. Preserve unresolved blocking requests; recording a question does not answer it. A null proposal cannot be accepted. Return the decision, concrete reasons, reconstructed answer, limits, strengths, findings, research requests and duration note. Acceptance applies to this version's suitability for writing.
