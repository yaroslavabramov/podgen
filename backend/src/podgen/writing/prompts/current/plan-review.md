# Review the proposed episode

Read the chosen story as written before checking it against the dossier. Does it serve the assignment and give this audience a reason to follow? Preserve an explicitly asked question; judge a selected question on how well it serves a topic request. `working_answer` should answer directly and `route_summary` should convey a connected course that stands on its own.

Read `opening.approach` as a brief retelling: does it contain the actual beginning and a reason to hear what follows? Read `route_summary` the same way, through the ending. Where either field switches to internal instructions, identify what to replace with events or explanation or remove, keeping chronology accurate. Preserve a working opening and the writer's choice of spoken style.

Follow the opening through the acts to the ending from what listeners would know along the way. Does each development make the next worth hearing, and does the ending deliver what the opening made interesting? Judge movement in the intended genre: events and consequences can carry a narrative or interview, an unfolding idea or mechanism an explanation. If the line cannot sustain its promise, request reconsideration of the route using the available material.

Acts should offer developments to tell or explain, with questions and transitions arising from the story. Background belongs where this episode needs orientation or meaning. Details and numbers may give presence, scale, stakes or explanatory value. Assess pace across the whole route and leave spoken composition to the writer. Technical prerequisites can help an explanation; narrative acts may leave them empty.

Check claims against the full research and request factual repairs where chronology, attribution or confidence would give listeners a false understanding. Require omitted context when a retained claim needs it to be intelligible and accurate or the client explicitly requires it. Optional suggestions may enrich a moment or sharpen its meaning; explain their contribution and leave their use to the writer. Completeness alone is no reason to add facts or statistics.

For findings, identify the problem, its effect, the needed change and useful material to preserve. Repair a false inference where it is created; moving a transition or choosing a more exact verb may be sufficient. Target the whole structure when the chosen line fails. Copy targets from `allowed_review_targets`, rooted at `review_target_root`, and give relevant support references.

Choose `accept` for a usable route with no required repairs or blocking research. Use `revise` for repairs possible with existing material, including an opaque synopsis or acts made of editorial instructions. Use `needs_research` for a specific evidence gap preventing an adequate version. Optional refinements allow acceptance. Return the decision, reasons, listener answer, strengths, findings, research requests and duration note.
