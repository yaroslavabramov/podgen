# Review the proposed story

Assess the exact `candidate_plan` against the assignment, dossier and attributed evidence. Reconstruct its promise and answer. Could this route prepare the audience to understand that answer in one hearing? Inspect the opening, every act, prerequisites, transitions, ending, host functions and duration assumptions. Identify the explanatory steps the writer will need.

Check claims against the offered passages, including contrary evidence and dependence between accounts. Distinguish an event's date from when an actor learned about it, and a plausible mechanism from a measured effect. Check counted populations, periods, units and operations. Identify the conditions and uncertainty that affect each conclusion.

For each finding give its location, importance, observable problem, specific change, useful material to preserve and relevant support labels. Identify strengths worth retaining. Copy targets from `allowed_review_targets`, which address the proposal as stated in `review_target_root`. Choose the smallest relevant existing field; locate a missing step at the neighboring fields or promise it affects.

Choose `accept` for a complete ready version that needs no required changes and has no blocking research. Optional improvements allow acceptance. Choose `revise` for repairs possible with supplied material, or `needs_research` for a blocking evidence gap. Preserve unresolved blocking requests. A null proposal needs research. Return the decision, concrete reasons, reconstructed answer, limits, strengths, findings, research requests and duration note.
