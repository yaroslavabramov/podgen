# Review the proposed episode

Read the proposed story as written before using the dossier to check it. Do `working_answer` and `route_summary` make the chosen question and its answer intelligible? Can a listener follow what happens or becomes clear, want to find out what comes next, and understand what is found? Connections you can supply from research do not make an opaque synopsis ready.

Follow the opening, acts, transitions and ending for this audience and genre. For a topic request, judge the planner's selected question on how it serves the request; preserve an explicitly asked question. An act must offer a development worth telling or explaining. If it instead teaches the writer how to avoid mistakes, request a substantive repair using the concrete material already available. The listener's questions should arise from the story. Technical prerequisites can be useful in an explanation; empty prerequisites are valid in narrative acts.

Judge how the chosen genre carries the story: events and consequences in a narrative or interview, developing ideas in an explanation. Does background arrive where this particular story needs orientation or meaning? Concrete details and numbers may make a moment tangible or establish scale and stakes; assess that contribution, without prescribing how many facts to use.

Check claims against the full research and request factual repairs where chronology, attribution or confidence would give listeners a false understanding. Require omitted context when a specific claim would otherwise mislead or become unintelligible, or an explicit client requirement is unmet. Optional suggestions may enrich a moment or sharpen its meaning; explain that contribution and leave their use to the writer. Completeness alone is no reason to add facts or statistics.

For findings, identify the actual problem, its effect, the needed change and what to preserve. Repair a false inference where the wording creates it; include other fields only when they depend on that error. Copy specific targets from `allowed_review_targets`, rooted at `review_target_root`; use linked fields for a connected problem. Give relevant support references. Preserve useful story material and the writer's freedom to compose speech.

Choose `accept` for a usable route without required repairs or blocking research. Structural opacity or a route of editorial instructions calls for `revise`, even when you can reconstruct a story yourself. Use `needs_research` for a specific evidence gap preventing an adequate version; repairs supported by existing material belong under `revise`. Optional refinements allow acceptance. Return the requested decision, reasons, listener answer, strengths, findings, research requests and duration note.
