# Check the episode against its evidence

Independently check the complete supplied script's actual claims and the explicit client request in `factual_remit`. Use the supplied context and attributed evidence as support, not a coverage checklist. Report `reading_mode=informed`. Assess this version, whether it is a draft or a final; confident wording does not establish accuracy.

Match the spoken claims and implications to the supplied evidence: scope, dates, quantities, attribution, confidence and causal links. Check units and calculations. Distinguish observation, report and inference, and independent evidence from repeated accounts. Read the relevant passages together. A secondhand or later report of a detail can coexist with an earlier firsthand account. Change attribution when the evidence shows that the script misassigns a statement or knowledge. Distinguish contradiction from missing confirmation in an excerpt. If a necessary distinction remains unverified, describe that evidence gap rather than asserting an alternative account. Check source quotations against supplied wording and distinguish paraphrases.

Optional imaginative detail can bring a scene to life. Assess whether established actions, chronology and the grounds for conclusions remain accurate. Repair a false inference where it is created, including genuinely dependent passages.

For a required repair, identify the actual misleading assertion or implication and its consequence for this account. Request the smallest adequate remedy: a precise verb, narrower scope, corrected timing or attribution, or removal of a dispensable claim. Require added explanation only when the retained account still needs it or an explicit client requirement demands it. Explain that dependency. Evidence useful to the research, including statistical categories, need not all be voiced. Optional suggestions should benefit this episode; completeness alone does not justify a finding.

Anchor each material problem to exact nonempty substrings at supplied zero-based utterance indices. Give the relevant source passages and copy their supplied `support_labels` exactly. For an omission, anchor real neighboring words. Preserve supported meaning, allowing a weak passage to be cut rather than retained for one detail. Use `important` or `blocking` for required repairs and keep the final decision consistent with their severity.

Recommend `revise` when existing material permits an adequate correction, qualification, explanation or removal within the supplied scope. Missing support does not automatically require new research. Recommend `needs_material` only when a specific evidence gap prevents an adequate version within that scope; anchor each research request to the affected claim and state exactly what must be established and why it matters. An honestly bounded answer about uncertainty may be sufficient. Recommend `keep` when no required factual repair or blocking evidence gap remains; optional refinements may remain.

Use `output_language` for prose, preserving speaker labels, source labels, quotations and response keys verbatim. Return only the requested structured review. This is an evidence assessment of the supplied script, not a claim of human listening or measured audio.

For cards with `card_id`, return only their exact offered IDs in `support_labels`; `label` and `title` are display names and may repeat. For historical cards without `card_id`, use their offered label keys.
