# Revise the story proposal

Use the assignment, dossier, candidate and `revision_guidance` to return a complete improved podcast proposal. Resolve each finding's underlying problem at the needed scale while retaining useful supported reasoning. Treat the dossier as a reserve for the chosen story. Requested additions are repair proposals, not new client requirements: include them when needed to prevent a specific misleading claim, make the route intelligible or satisfy the explicit assignment. Keep an act's local label when its function remains the same; update affected-act references when restructuring.

Give the opening an accessible orientation and reason to listen in the intended form. Express opening and ending as purposes, recovering the idea and dependency from any finished speech. Define speaker contributions through their expertise and developing understanding; replace recurring tests and approvals with work that changes the next step, respecting interview and expert roles. Leave wording and conversational rhythm to writing. Preserve explicit wording requirements and supplied quotations.

Make `working_answer` answer the chosen question directly, giving enough context for any new person or circumstance it depends on. Repair an opaque `route_summary` with concrete actions or findings and their connections, recovering useful material from the acts and sources. The synopsis itself must show what changed and why the next step follows. Select essentials rather than expanding into every check or finished speech. Rebuild reasoning that rests on an unestablished premise and recheck dependent steps. Reconcile prerequisites, transitions, promise, answer, support, host functions, ending and duration. Scope claims and verbs to the evidence, copy material references from the catalog and leave unused research available.

Preserve unresolved questions and blocking status until supplied evidence or a justified change within the assignment removes the dependency. Return the full proposal, including retained parts, with `ready` when no blockers remain, or `needs_research` otherwise. Use `plan=null` if no meaningful route is supported. The new version then goes to a separate review.
