# Propose the story

Distinguish the question the episode must answer from what this audience can already recognize or reasonably ask. In `main_question`, preserve the requested problem while expressing it in words this audience can understand. A required title remains an exact assignment condition. Choose your route independently of the order of the brief or cards. In `route_summary`, show what intelligible observation, conflict or reasoning gives rise to the central question, what each step establishes, and why the next question follows. Prepare concepts before the reasoning depends on them. State the listener promise and supported working answer.

Use the supplied expertise and intended form: direct explanation, a scene or a purposeful mystery can work without an everyday example.

Specify each act's listener question, intended change, prerequisites, supporting cards, relevant conditions and transitions. Describe the opening's purpose and the understanding the ending should leave, in planning language rather than finished speech. Put the audience's entry point in `opening.approach` and the essential explanatory links in the acts and transitions, so the writer has these supports without a copy of `route_summary`. Define each host's intellectual contribution. In dialogue, plan useful work that builds on the partner's discoveries rather than a reaction to repeat after every question. The author will choose the spoken wording. Preserve any exact wording explicitly required by the assignment and identify supplied source quotations as such. Copy `material_refs` from the supplied catalog and use short local act labels.

Estimate act and total duration as ranges with assumptions, counting the opening, transitions and ending once. Identify optional substance that can be cut while keeping the explanatory chain.

Return the complete proposal, limitations and unresolved research questions. For each question state why it matters, affected acts, relevant offered support and whether it blocks the answer. Research requests record unmet needs. Use `status=ready` for a complete proposal without blocking questions, or `needs_research` otherwise. Use `plan=null` if the material cannot support a meaningful route. A supported answer about uncertainty can be ready. The proposed version then goes to a separate editor.
