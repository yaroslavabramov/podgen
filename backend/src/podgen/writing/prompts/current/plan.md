# Propose the story

In `main_question`, express the requested problem in words this audience can understand. Preserve any required title exactly. Choose a route through facts in `dossier` and its cards independently of their order. In `route_summary`, show what recognizable observation, conflict or reasoning motivates the central question, what each step establishes and why the next follows. Prepare concepts before using them. State the listener promise and supported working answer.

Give the opening an accessible entry and reason to listen: direct orientation, a recognizable observation, a grounded scene or an intelligible question can work. Choose for the subject, audience and intended form; a mystery, greeting or anecdote is optional. Ground actual personal stories in supplied material; a general situation can start from a recognizable observation.

Specify each act's listener question, intended change, prerequisites, supporting cards, conditions and transitions. Put the audience's entry point in `opening.approach` and essential explanatory links in acts and transitions; the writer will not receive `route_summary`. Describe opening and ending purposes in planning language, leaving spoken wording to the author. Define speakers' contributions from their expertise and developing understanding, with room for either to change the next step. Preserve intended interview or expert roles without prescribing a recurring test, approval or reaction. Preserve explicit wording requirements and identify supplied quotations. Copy `material_refs` from the catalog and use short local act labels.

Estimate act and total duration as ranges with assumptions, counting opening, transitions and ending once. Identify optional substance that can be cut while preserving the explanation.

Return the complete proposal, limitations and unmet research needs. For each question state why it matters, affected acts, relevant offered support and whether it blocks the answer. Use `status=ready` without blockers, or `needs_research` otherwise; `plan=null` if no meaningful route is supported. An evidenced answer about uncertainty can be ready. The proposal goes to separate review.
