# Plan the episode

Find an episode in the dossier that this audience will want to follow. Preserve an explicitly asked question; for a topic request, choose a question the material makes worth answering.

Before dividing it into acts, explore the different stories the material could support when there is a real choice. Look for questions, decisions, discoveries or mechanisms that could carry the episode. Mentally follow each promising opening through what happens or becomes clear, its consequences and its ending. What would listeners know at each point, why would they want to continue, and does the material support the promised answer? Choose the line whose development and payoff best serve the assignment, audience and duration. Develop a clear existing route directly. An explanatory episode can follow an unfolding idea or mechanism to an answer.

Return the chosen route as a complete proposal. State its question in `main_question` and answer it directly in `working_answer`, introducing people or circumstances needed to understand it. Make `listener_promise` express the reason to follow. In `opening.approach`, briefly retell the opening in your own words: what listeners encounter, what they learn or notice next, and the question this raises. In `route_summary`, carry that retelling through the ending. Across the plan, express the chosen account through what people do, what happens or how something works. Include conditions and uncertainty that affect the meaning of what is said. The writer chooses spoken wording and rhythm.

Each act's `intended_change_in_understanding` states what becomes clear from its events or reasoning. Transitions and listener questions grow from that development. Place background where this story needs orientation or meaning. Choose details and numbers for presence, scale, stakes or explanatory value. Use `prerequisite_concepts` for technical knowledge the reasoning needs; narrative acts may leave it empty. State what the ending resolves.

Describe each host's part in that account. In solo, say what the narrator tells or explains; in dialogue, what each speaker brings through their knowledge, questions and responses. Let `interaction_guidance` develop those contributions and relationships. Copy `material_refs` from the catalog. Estimate act and total duration as ranges with assumptions, counting opening, transitions and ending once; identify optional material.

Return unmet research needs with the proposal. Explain what is missing, why the chosen route needs it, affected acts, offered support and whether it blocks an adequate answer. Use `ready` without blockers, `needs_research` otherwise, and `plan=null` if no meaningful route is supported. A supported answer about uncertainty can be ready.
