# Propose the story

Plan a podcast, choosing a story and details that serve the user's request for this audience and duration. For a topic request, find an interesting central question in the sources; preserve a question the user explicitly asked. Express it intelligibly in `main_question`, preserving any explicitly required title. The `dossier` is a reserve of material; neither it nor selected cards are a coverage checklist. Choose independently of their order. In `route_summary`, show what motivates the question, what the chosen steps establish and why they connect. Prepare concepts the selected reasoning needs. State the listener promise and supported working answer.

Give the opening an accessible entry and reason to listen: direct orientation, a recognizable observation, a grounded scene or an intelligible question can work. Choose for the subject, audience and intended form; a mystery, greeting or anecdote is optional. Ground actual personal stories in supplied material; a general situation can start from a recognizable observation.

Specify each act's listener question, intended change, prerequisites, supporting cards and transitions. Put the audience's entry point in `opening.approach` and essential explanatory links in acts and transitions; the writer will not receive `route_summary`. Scope intended claims and verbs to their evidence. Describe opening and ending purposes in planning language, leaving spoken wording to the author. Define speakers' contributions from their expertise and developing understanding, with room for either to change the next step. Preserve intended interview or expert roles without prescribing a recurring test, approval or reaction. Preserve explicit wording requirements and identify supplied quotations. Copy `material_refs` from the catalog and use short local act labels.

Estimate act and total duration as ranges with assumptions, counting opening, transitions and ending once. Identify optional substance that can be cut while preserving the explanation.

Return the complete proposal and unmet research needs. For each question state why it matters, affected acts, relevant offered support and whether it blocks the answer. Use `status=ready` without blockers, or `needs_research` otherwise; `plan=null` if no meaningful route is supported. An evidenced answer about uncertainty can be ready. The proposal goes to separate review.
