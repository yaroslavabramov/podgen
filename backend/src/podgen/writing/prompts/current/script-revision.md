# Revise the spoken episode

Use the assignment, complete draft, selected support and `revision_guidance` to revise the podcast. Resolve required problems at their proper scale. Preserve useful speech and explicit client requirements. For a factual finding, distinguish misleading meaning from the proposed means of repair. Its `requested_change` proposes a means, and `preserve` identifies useful contributions rather than making every listed detail mandatory. Verify corrections against the supplied evidence; a review does not establish a new fact. Add explanation when the retained account still needs it. Choose optional additions for their contribution to this episode.

Use `editorial_frame.route_summary` to understand the proposed course; it may describe an earlier arrangement. Its central question, promise, order and selection remain working decisions, not obligations to restore. The `positive_observations` in `revision_guidance` give both a quotation and the reason it worked. Preserve that contribution in its new context; praise of accuracy does not require retaining the same spoken explanation of it. Use `duration_context` to judge current scale against the target and range, allowing natural variation in delivery.

When both correction and removal can resolve a dispensable claim, compare their effect on the passage. What does keeping it give the listener: necessary reasoning, scale, stakes, character or pleasure? What attention does its accurate framing cost: new categories, dates, repetition or an interruption of the live action? Choose the repair whose contribution earns that cost in this position. Precision can itself be the discovery or carry the stakes; deletion is not the default. Judge economy by the resulting listening experience, not by how many original words survive. Recheck dependent comparisons and calculations after either choice.

In `targeted` mode, `revision_operation.targets` are permitted locations, not a checklist. Choose replacement, deletion or insertion for what the repaired passage needs. Read each proposed edit with its unchanged neighbors; a technically local replacement can still overload a scene or repeat an explanation elsewhere.

Repair a connected pattern across its whole exchange, sequence or route. Consider removing a weak passage completely and joining the surviving account. An interesting detail alone need not preserve it. Place background where this story needs it and retain details for presence, meaning or pleasure. Consolidate repeated endings. In dialogue, change how contributions affect responses, keeping plausible knowledge and useful expert roles. A favorable review does not freeze an old passage in its new context. If a necessary premise is missing, choose a supported entry or keep the specific research need explicit; do not invent a motivation to connect the facts.

Rewrite for the experience of listening: idiomatic speech, concrete images, active verbs, varied cadence and a voice with an attitude toward this subject. Improve a stiff or stalled sequence at the necessary scale. Choose, combine or move dispensable material before expanding it with explanations; make room when an addition is essential. Let a teaser preserve both orientation and discovery, and let the developments during a delayed answer sustain interest. Humor, asides, direct address, callbacks and reflective comments may give character, insight or pleasure. Keep their effective timing rather than deleting the device. Do not turn a joke into an earnest disclaimer, certify responsible storytelling in the narration or rebut an interpretation the account neither states nor implies. Preserve useful texture and breathing room along with the supported meaning.

Track discovery inside each scene and its earlier previews and transitions: when does the connection first become knowable, and what does the rest offer? Restore the encounter with clues where an early explanation has spent it, repairing the first disclosure and dependent handoffs. A teaser can name an object or outcome while saving another worthwhile discovery. Source cautions need not become narration; accurate attribution or framing may suffice. Keep retained quantities accurate.

When `craft_reference` is supplied, study how its revised passages change selection, order, voice and the space given to a scene. Follow the editorial reasoning in the comparisons, using this episode's own evidence and language. The examples' fictional events and wording belong to those examples.

Read the complete result as speech, including retained turns. Check the opening from what listeners know then, the new joins, genre and ending. Track what has already been revealed and what each later scene still offers. After moving passages, verify dates, attribution and who knew what, and follow changed numbers into later inferences. Reconsider the resulting composition as well as its chronology. Let listeners connect facts when the connection is already clear. Preserve source quotations and assigned speaker labels exactly. Shape the complete telling for the assigned duration. Keep research needs explicit where a retained claim or required answer depends on them.

Follow `revision_operation.mode` when supplied and the requested response structure when returning edits or a complete revision. Use assigned speaker labels and put only words to be spoken and punctuation in utterance `text`.
