# Edit for one listening

Would you send this episode to the assigned speakers to record? Read the words once in playback order, for the stated audience and genre. Judge what this telling gives a listener. Report `reading_mode=script_only`, write prose in `output_language` and preserve the supplied labels, quotes and keys.

## Follow the story

At a decisive action, what has the listener heard that makes the choice intelligible? Separate a missing piece of specialist knowledge from an ordinary inference the audience can already make. If the move depends on a fact only a specialist would know, identify it and the exact point of need. An unanswered question can create suspense when listeners know why it matters. Once they have enough to follow, let them make the connection. If a passage belabors it, try a shorter telling of the connected block. Which observation, comparison or incident lets the next beat work? Keep details that change the conclusion or give the scene life.

Hear the strongest promise before a change of scene against what the next scene actually delivers. Consider the ending in the context of the whole episode. What did we follow, what changed, and where do we get to experience that change? Choose the ending you would record. Keep one that lands. If a recap buries a good last moment, try cutting or reordering the lead-in. If the words run out before the story arrives anywhere, commission the missing ending: name the unfinished movement and what the writer needs to accomplish using material they have. Allow a newly composed final passage. Keep the choice of words with the writer and ground reported events in their sources. A list of conclusions, the last available fact and an open question each need to earn their place here.

## Hear the speaker

What is the speaker doing in this moment? Hear the setup, wording and timing of an aside. A joke may work because of the gap between its literal words and what the listener recognizes. For a serious correction, identify the actual belief or implication in the preceding speech. If the correction introduces its own target, try the passage without it. An imagined funny delivery cannot supply missing wit on the page. Judge exchanges in light of who is speaking.

Hear case, agreement, idiom and cadence. An objective grammatical error needs an important local correction. Deliberate conversational phrasing can work. Around an action, mentally remove the surrounding phrases and hear what disappears: a new thought, a concrete image, a joke, tension, or another explanation of something already heard? Figurative language is not a defect by itself.

## Give the writer an executable edit

For each required change, quote the words it concerns, name the listening effect, describe the editorial job, and say what should survive. Give one coherent assignment when a scene needs rebuilding. For sequence scope, two short exact quotes in `sequence_ranges` identify the first and last editable utterances, inclusive. They may be in the same utterance. Include the full passage the writer should reshape; for a missing close, authorize the final passage where the ending belongs. For a local deletion, quote the complete wording to remove. Quoting only the words on either side does not authorize their removal or a rewrite of the intervening speech. Address dependent passages if the new join changes them.

If you would accept the passage for recording only after this change, mark it important or blocking and recommend revise. Use optional for alternatives equally acceptable for recording. Use `keep` when no required repair remains. Record the reason to preserve a strong choice in strengths. Anchor findings with exact quotations and zero-based indices. Use duration estimates for pace. Leave `research_requests` empty. The evidence editor handles verification.
