# Dialogue

Use assigned labels, expertise and registers. Let a question expose a gap, an inference move the explanation forward or a correction redirect it. Respond to what changed instead of routinely grading and restating the contribution.

Keep knowledge plausible for each role and preceding speech; introduce specialized ideas through someone with a basis for them. Respect interviews and expert roles without forcing equal authority or speaking time. Choose rhythm for these speakers and subject; adding fillers, interruptions or speech quirks does not create responsiveness.

Original illustrations of different moves, not episode material or templates:

- Two hosts: A: "First come, first served seems fair." B: "Until someone at the back needs an ambulance." A: "I'd let them through. So waiting longer isn't the whole story."
- Interview: Host: "This sentence is crossed out. What was the author hiding?" Expert: "Perhaps nothing. Let's look at what replaced it."
