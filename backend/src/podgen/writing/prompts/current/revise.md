Preserve useful speech and explicit client requirements. Requested additions are proposed remedies, not new requirements. A precise sentence, narrower claim or deletion may settle a factual problem. Verify corrections against the supplied evidence; a review does not establish a new fact. Add explanation when the retained account still needs it. Choose optional additions for their contribution to this episode.

Follow the episode's course in `editorial_frame.route_summary`. The `positive_observations` in `revision_guidance` give both a quotation and the reason it worked. Preserve that contribution in its new context. Use `duration_context` to judge current scale against the target and range, allowing natural variation in delivery.

Repair a connected pattern across its whole exchange or closing sequence. Consider removing a weak passage completely and joining the surviving account. An interesting detail alone need not preserve it. Place background where this story needs it and retain details for presence, meaning or pleasure. Consolidate repeated endings. In dialogue, change how contributions affect responses, keeping plausible knowledge and useful expert roles. A favorable review does not freeze an old passage in its new context. Realize the plan's purpose in your own speech.

Rewrite for the experience of listening: idiomatic speech, concrete images, active verbs, varied cadence and a voice with an attitude toward this subject. Improve a stiff or stalled sequence at the necessary scale. Choose, combine or move dispensable material before expanding it with explanations; make room when an addition is essential. Let a teaser preserve both orientation and discovery, and let the developments during a delayed answer sustain interest. Humor, asides, direct address, callbacks and reflective comments may give character, insight or pleasure. Keep their effective timing rather than deleting the device. Do not turn a joke into an earnest disclaimer, certify responsible storytelling in the narration or rebut an interpretation the account neither states nor implies. Preserve useful texture and breathing room along with the supported meaning.

Original fictional editorial illustrations:

- An archivist finds a builder's address in the twelfth catalogue just before the library closes. "She had been through eleven catalogues. The twelfth gave her an address, just as the library was closing."
  Choice: retain the effort and discovery, cut the catalogue descriptions, and leave time to follow the address.
- Fictional records: the bridge opened in May; engineers found a crack beneath its western arch during an inspection in June. Before: "The inspection found a crack before the bridge opened." Revision: "During the June inspection, engineers found a crack beneath the western arch of a bridge that had opened in May."
  Choice: date and observer correct the false sequence within the action. The repair needs no subsequent warning.
