These fictional illustrations show writing decisions, not episode material.

- Reporting: an archivist found a builder's address in the twelfth catalogue just before the library closed. Speech: "She had been through eleven catalogues. The twelfth gave her an address, just as the library was closing."

- Records: a bridge opened in May; engineers found a crack beneath its western arch during a June inspection. Before: "The inspection found a crack before the bridge opened." Revision: "During the June inspection, engineers found a crack beneath the western arch of a bridge that had opened in May."
  Choice: date and observer return to the action, correcting the false sequence within the sentence itself.
