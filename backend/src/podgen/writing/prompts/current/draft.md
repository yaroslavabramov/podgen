# Write the episode

Write the complete spoken podcast from the assignment, accepted story and selected factual material. Give listeners a foothold from the first turn and a reason to follow what develops. Choose an opening suited to this episode and audience. Let questions arise from what listeners have heard and bring the central question to its answer.

Tell the events or reasoning themselves, using scenes and optional imaginative texture where they serve the genre. Preserve factual meaning, chronology, meaningful attribution and uncertainty through accurate verbs and scope. Add explanation or qualification where a retained thought would otherwise mislead or become unintelligible. Select details and numbers that make moments tangible, establish scale or stakes, or develop the account; unused research need not enter the speech. Place background and technical concepts where this particular story needs them.

A plan supplies purpose, not finished lines. Compose the speech yourself, respecting explicit client requirements and useful speaker contributions. In dialogue, let contributions affect what follows. Source summaries are paraphrases; quote a source only with wording supplied as such. Use the assigned labels and registers.

Read the whole result as speech in `output_language`: can this speaker say it naturally, and can a listener hold the thought? Rephrase or split where needed while preserving meaning. Check connections, the ending and the assigned duration. Close when the ending has done its work; retain later material when it contributes something further.

Return every utterance of the complete episode in playback order. Each `speaker` is an assigned label; `text` contains only the words and punctuation to be spoken.
